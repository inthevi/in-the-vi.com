St. Thomas Travel Guide

The vibrant heart and main hub of the US Virgin Islands

St. Thomas is the busiest and most developed of the US Virgin Islands. It serves as the main gateway with excellent shopping, beautiful beaches, and easy ferry access to St. John and the BVI.

Best Beaches

Magens Bay, Coki Beach, Sapphire Beach, and Lindquist Beach are highlights.

Where to Eat

Long-standing favorites include Duffy's Love Shack in Red Hook.
