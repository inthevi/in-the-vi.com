St. John Travel Guide

The most pristine and protected island in the US Virgin Islands

St. John is widely regarded as the crown jewel of the US Virgin Islands. More than two-thirds of the island is protected as Virgin Islands National Park.

Best Beaches on St. John

Trunk Bay, Cinnamon Bay, Hawksnest Bay, and Waterlemon Cay (excellent for turtles) are highlights.

Snorkeling & Marine Life

A good mask and fins make a big difference. Waterlemon Cay is legendary for sea turtles.

Where to Eat

Long-standing favorites include Skinny Legs in Coral Bay and Woody’s in Cruz Bay.
